# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 5, 2026**

## Overview

ZueriRatsinfo is an automated bot that fetches publicly available vote results from the Zurich City Council (Gemeinderat Stadt Zürich) and the Zurich Cantonal Council (Kantonsrat Zürich) and publishes them to social media platforms including X (Twitter), Bluesky, Instagram, and Facebook.

This privacy policy applies to the ZueriRatsinfo application and its associated Meta Developer App used for Instagram publishing.

## Data Collection

ZueriRatsinfo does **not** collect, store, or process any personal data from users or visitors.

- The app does not have user-facing login or registration
- The app does not use cookies or tracking technologies
- The app does not collect IP addresses, device information, or usage analytics
- The app does not share any data with third parties

## Data Published

The only data published by this bot is publicly available council vote records, sourced from open government data:

- **Gemeinderat Stadt Zürich** — the [City of Zurich PARIS API](https://opendatazurich.github.io/paris-api/)
- **Kantonsrat Zürich** — [OpenParlData](https://api.openparldata.ch), published under CC BY 4.0
- **Nationalrat** — the [Parlamentsdienste's open data web services](https://www.parlament.ch/de/über-das-parlament/fakten-und-zahlen/open-data-web-services)

Every post names its source on a "Daten: …" line: Stadt Zürich, OpenParlData.ch, or Parlamentsdienste der Bundesversammlung, Bern. OpenParlData's licence and the Parlamentsdienste's terms require that credit.

All include names of elected council members acting in their official public capacity.

## Social Media Platforms

This app uses the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) API solely to publish content to the ZueriRatsinfo Instagram account. No data from Meta users is accessed, stored, or processed beyond what is required for authentication of the bot's own account.
